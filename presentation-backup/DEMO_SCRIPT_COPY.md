# SHEHARNAAMA — 90-Second Live Demonstration Script

Exact clicks, scrolls and speaking notes for the Brandathon Round 3 demo.
Everything shown is real, implemented behaviour — nothing here pretends the
business has launched or that a booking was made.

Before starting:
- Open `http://localhost:8080` (or the published URL) in a fresh private window.
- Set the browser zoom to 100% and hide bookmarks bar for a clean look.
- Optionally pre-clear localStorage so the form demo starts clean.

---

## 0:00–0:12 — The hero (do not scroll yet)

**Show:** full hero — eyebrow, headline, both CTAs, the three pilot facts.

**Say:**
> "Every visitor to Pune sees Shaniwar Wada, Dagdusheth, the famous plaques.
> Sheharnaama is a heritage-walking experience for everything in between —
> the lanes, doorways, crafts and street life between the landmarks.
> Our line is simple: *Find the stories between the landmarks.*"

---

## 0:12–0:25 — Scroll to the Peth Stories Walk

**Do:** click **"Explore the Walk"** (primary CTA) — the page scrolls to the
dark featured-experience section.

**Say:**
> "Our pilot chapter is the *Peth Stories Walk* — a proposed 90-to-120-minute
> walk through old Pune's Peth neighbourhoods, for small groups of 8 to 12.
> Note the transparency: duration, group size and the indicative ₹599 pilot
> price are all clearly labelled as proposals being validated — we're not
> pretending tickets exist."

**Point out:** the five "possible elements" and the Story Passport mention.

---

## 0:25–0:40 — The five-stage sample route

**Do:** scroll down to **"Every stop opens another story"**.

**Say:**
> "Here's the shape of the walk, as a sample route — meeting point,
> architectural details, everyday stories, a cultural pause, and collecting
> the story. The map is deliberately illustrative: the real route and
> meeting point get confirmed before any walk is offered. No fake GPS, no
> invented timings."

---

## 0:40–0:55 — The Story Passport (the differentiator)

**Do:** continue to the indigo **Story Passport** section and click
**"Look inside"** (the passport flips). Click **"View cover"** to flip back.

**Say:**
> "This is what makes Sheharnaama more than a walk: the Story Passport —
> a journal-inspired keepsake. Story stamps mark the moments, memory notes
> capture a word or sketch, and QR links can connect a physical page to
> further reading. The mockup shows a sample QR placeholder — clearly
> labelled, because a real link isn't configured yet."

---

## 0:55–1:10 — The #WhatLiesBetween campaign

**Do:** scroll to the terracotta campaign section.

**Say:**
> "Our campaign line: *You've seen the landmark — have you heard the lane?*
> #WhatLiesBetween gives the brand a content engine: the doorway, the craft,
> the lane. These three cards are our first poster series."

---

## 1:10–1:35 — The interest form and the real registration flow

**Do:** scroll to **"Be part of the first chapter"**.

1. Click **"Register My Interest"** with everything empty — inline errors
   appear and focus jumps to the first one.
2. Fill clearly fictional details: name `Demo Visitor`, email
   `demo@example.com`, guests `2`, experience `Peth Stories Walk`,
   tick consent.
3. Submit — the success message shows a **server-generated reference**
   (e.g. `SH-2026-4C9A1B`).

**Say:**
> "Validation works end to end — required fields, email format, consent.
> This submission goes to a real backend and lands in a database — and the
> reference you see was generated by that server, live. Note the wording:
> this is an *expression of interest*, not a confirmed tour booking.
> Availability and final details will be communicated separately."

## 1:35–1:50 — The admin console (optional but impressive)

**Do:** open a second tab to `/admin`, sign in with the local demo
credentials, find the same reference in the table, open it, set status to
**Contacted**, save.

**Say:**
> "Every submission lands in a protected admin console — real counts,
> search, status workflow and CSV export, all behind server-side
> authentication. The team sees exactly what visitors asked for, including
> accessibility notes, so walks can be planned responsibly."

---

## 1:50 — Close on the final CTA

**Do:** scroll to the dusk-skyline closing section.

**Say:**
> "Sheharnaama: take the slower road, notice the details, and find the
> stories between the landmarks. *#WhatLiesBetween.* Thank you."

---

## Click/scroll order (cheat sheet)

| Step | Action | Lands on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wait on load | Hero |
| 2 | Click "Explore the Walk" | Peth Stories Walk |
| 3 | Scroll | Sample route (5 stages) |
| 4 | Scroll → click "Look inside" → "View cover" | Story Passport flip |
| 5 | Scroll | #WhatLiesBetween campaign |
| 6 | Scroll → submit empty → fill → submit | Form validation + server reference |
| 7 | Second tab: /admin → login → update status | Admin console |
| 8 | Scroll to end | Final CTA |

Requires the full-stack server: `venv\Scripts\python -m uvicorn backend.app.main:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8080` (see README_FULLSTACK.md).

## If something goes wrong live
- Page won't load → open `index.html` directly from the folder (offline mode).
- Fonts look different → that's the fallback stack; the demo still works.
- Form misbehaves → narrate the inline errors themselves; they demonstrate
  the validation requirement.
