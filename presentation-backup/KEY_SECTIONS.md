# Website Key Sections — Presenter's Cheat Sheet

One-page map of the Sheharnaama landing page (top to bottom). All sections
exist and were verified in the current build.

| # | Section | What it shows | Why it matters to judges |
|---|---------|---------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Sticky header | Original arch logo (user-supplied `logo_icon.svg`), nav, "Join the First Walk" CTA, mobile hamburger | Immediate brand identity; working navigation |
| 2 | Hero | "The city is more than its landmarks." + illustrated old-Pune lane, pilot facts (90–120 min, 8–12 guests, Story Passport) labelled *proposed* | Core idea lands in one screen |
| 3 | Brand manifesto ("Our Story") | "The magic is in-between." + Architecture / People & Craft / Food & Everyday Life, with consent-and-arrangement caveats | Brand thinking, not just a tour page |
| 4 | Featured experience | Peth Stories Walk card: location, proposed duration/group size, format, possible elements, indicative pilot price ₹599 labelled "proposed, subject to validation" | The product, with honest pilot labelling |
| 5 | Sample route | Five numbered stages + illustrative route map (captioned "not a navigational map") | Experience design without fake GPS claims |
| 6 | How it works | 4 steps: choose → meet storyteller → experience → keep the memory | Clear first-time explanation |
| 7 | Story Passport | Flippable mockup (cover ⇄ inside), Story Stamps / Memory Notes / QR Story Links, sample QR placeholder clearly labelled | The key brand differentiator |
| 8 | Why Sheharnaama | Respectful comparison vs conventional sightseeing + closing line | Positioning |
| 9 | Who it is for | City Rediscoverer / Culture Seeker / Shared Experience panels | Audience segmentation |
| 10 | #WhatLiesBetween campaign | Campaign line, hashtag stamp, three poster cards (Doorway / Craft / Lane) | Marketing & content engine |
| 11 | Gallery | Six illustrated brand images with captions and lightbox | Visual polish |
| 12 | Registration | Validated demo form (name, email, optional date, guests 1–12, experience, notes, consent) with honest "no backend / no booking" response | Conversion + integrity |
| 13 | FAQ | 8 accessible accordions matching the pilot concept | Objection handling |
| 14 | Final CTA | Dusk-skyline closer with both CTAs | Emotional close |
| 15 | Footer | Logo, tagline, nav, privacy note, unconfigured contact placeholder, hashtag, dynamic year | Professional finish |
